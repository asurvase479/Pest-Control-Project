# Pest-Control-Project using Power BI 

End-to-end automated Marketplace Analytics solution(Volume Analysis) built with Python, Keepa API, and Power BI, replacing manual data collection, cost calculation, and reporting across 961 ASINs and $352M in annual revenue.

<img width="1257" height="707" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8a2ad4ce-9233-47e5-9b15-494f042a4487" />

Interactive slicers dynamically update all KPIs and visuals, giving instant insights by brand, category, and sub-category.

<img width="1271" height="722" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/01d74b8a-e758-4ac9-996c-41c0c6ff3572" />

The Target Cost Analysis dashboard automatically calculates the maximum product cost for each ASIN that still gives a 20% or 25% profit margin. It uses the current selling price and fees such as freight, duty, Amazon referral and FBA fees, storage, returns and marketing, and the assumptions can be adjusted in the base data.
Slicers for brand, category and market (USA or Non-USA) filter the table instantly, and a negative target cost shows that a product can’t reach the profit goal at its current price. This replaces the manual per-product costing sheets and helps with sourcing and pricing decisions.

<img width="1262" height="712" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1ca80a36-60f7-417b-b1f3-75b25a83c448" />

The Manufacture Analysis dashboard gives one searchable view of which manufacturers are linked to each brand, along with their master category and sub-category. For example, Bonide is mapped to 18 manufacturers across 10 main categories and 11 sub-categories. It also lists each manufacturer's address, phone number and email, so the team can contact them directly.Slicers for brand, manufacturer, master category and sub-category filter the table and KPIs instantly. This replaces the manual web research and scattered spreadsheets otherwise needed to find manufacturer details, and it speeds up sourcing and vendor outreach.

<img width="1252" height="710" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f8ed488b-ffa4-4290-a16b-e9439891f735" />

This dashboard ranks brands by annual and average monthly revenue, such as ZEVO at about $86M, Scotts at $70M and Syngenta at $37M. It also breaks revenue down by master category and sub-category, for example Cockroach Bait & Poison at about $19.7M. This makes it easy to spot top brands and strong categories without building manual reports each week.

<img width="1260" height="697" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a116f85a-94d2-46e0-8c2b-3ccd28027e19" />

This dashboard lets you type a keyword (like “spray”) and instantly see matching ASINs with their brand, sub-category, title and annual revenue. In the example, the Bonide spray products add up to about $34M in annual revenue. It replaces manual filtering and searching through large product sheets, so product research is much faster.

<img width="1252" height="702" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/08894174-41b9-44de-960a-438219b4cb0a" />

This dashboard keeps all supplier details in one place: supplier name, country of origin, products, website, email and phone. Slicers for supplier, country and source filter the list, and KPIs show the total suppliers, countries and product types. It replaces scattered vendor spreadsheets and makes sourcing outreach quicker.
